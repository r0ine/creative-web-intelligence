# Source Research Prompt v4

Given a missing design resource:
1. state what is missing and why external research is justified;
2. select up to three compatible sources from v4 registry;
3. check access/automation policy before querying;
4. retrieve metadata only through documented channels;
5. normalize candidates;
6. reject candidates with unclear licenses or weak Design DNA fit;
7. output provenance for selected candidates;
8. do not bulk-download or mirror catalogs.
