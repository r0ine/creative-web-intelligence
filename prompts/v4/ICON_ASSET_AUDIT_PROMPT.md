# Icon / Asset Audit Prompt

Audit a completed UI without changing functionality.

Check:
- icon necessity and semantic clarity
- family consistency
- stroke/geometry/typography relationship
- icon sizing and hit targets
- animated icon purpose and reduced motion
- illustration/3D/Lottie/Rive purpose
- source and license provenance
- generic AI fingerprints

Remove assets before replacing them when they do not have a job.
