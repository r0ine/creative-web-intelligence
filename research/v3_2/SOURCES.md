- Accessibility cross-check: `https://colorcombinations.org/learn/accessible-palettes/`

### Evidence rules

- VERIFIED: structure/counts observed in primary/open dataset documentation.
- CONVERSION-SPECIFIC: RGB/HEX/Lab values generated from a particular CMYK-to-screen conversion; never label them as the unique historical truth.
- DERIVED: OKLCH, contrast, role scores and web suitability computed by our analyzer.
- SYNTHESIZED: design guidance derived from measured relationships plus web constraints.
