# Gallery Watchlist

## Primary
- Awwwards current winners + Sites of the Year: award-quality creative direction and dev.
- Siteinspire: large categorized corpus useful for typography, grid, unusual layouts and art direction.
- Hoverstat.es: alternative/experimental web; useful to escape template convergence.
- Codrops: implementation-focused creative development.
- Refs.gallery 3D/WebGL: smaller curated reference set.

## Research rule
A gallery inclusion is not proof a design pattern is universally good. Record the project context and the reason a pattern works before converting it into a recipe.
