# Studio / Gallery Benchmark Notes

## Lusion
Use as a benchmark for integrating 3D visual storytelling, motion and development as one experience. Do not copy its visual identity.

## Immersive Garden
Use as a benchmark for immersive case-study art direction and responsive presentation.

## Active Theory
Use as a benchmark for story + art + technology and interactive creative-tech thinking.

## Resn
Use as a benchmark for experimental WebGL/R&D behavior and expressive interaction.

## Locomotive
Use as a benchmark for joining art direction/UX/web design/development rather than treating animation as an add-on.

## Awwwards / CSSDA / Godly / Codrops
Use as discovery feeds. A gallery listing is not enough to infer exact implementation. Add a site to the deep-dive catalog only after its behavior can be observed directly or from a reliable case study.
