## Reusable principles extracted
- Build near-black palettes rather than assuming #000 is the only dark neutral.
- Treat font pairing as a role/contrast problem, not merely two trendy names.
- Maintain a catalog of distinctive display faces, but validate language coverage, licensing, loading cost and readability.
- Store color combinations as palette archetypes with contrast metadata, not as fixed mandatory brand kits.
- Never encode a creator’s exact aesthetic as the library default.
