# Pin, Scrub and Section Handoffs

Pinning creates temporary viewport ownership. Every pinned chapter must define release behavior.

A good handoff answers:
- what remains visually continuous?
- what disappears first?
- what becomes the next anchor?
- does background/color ownership crossfade or cut?
- does the next section enter while previous is pinned or after release?

Avoid abrupt jumps caused by spacer heights changing after media/font load. Recalculate after relevant layout changes.
