# Mobile Scroll Recomposition

Desktop choreography is not automatically touch-friendly.

On small screens consider:
- shorten or remove long pins
- replace horizontal scrollytelling with vertical chapters
- replace scrubbed tiny text with discrete states
- reduce 3D/canvas resolution/effects
- preserve native scrolling momentum
- keep tap targets stable
- ensure URL bars/viewport-height changes do not break pinned layouts
- provide static/short transition alternatives for reduced motion
