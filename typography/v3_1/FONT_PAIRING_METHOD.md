# Font Pairing Method

Pair by role and contrast, not popularity.

Useful contrast dimensions:
- serif vs sans
- humanist vs geometric
- narrow vs normal width
- high vs low stroke contrast
- expressive display vs neutral reading face

Useful compatibility dimensions:
- similar x-height/scale perception
- punctuation/numeric harmony
- shared historical/industrial/editorial tone
- compatible language coverage
- compatible licensing/loading model

Reject pairings that fight for attention or require excessive manual tracking/scale correction to coexist.
